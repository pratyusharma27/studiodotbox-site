The educator

Ar. Pratyush Sharma.

I am an architect and the founder of Studio Dotbox, a founder-led practice in Greater Noida working across residential, commercial, retail, hospitality and urban planning. I have spent over eight years in practice: at the drawing board, on site, and across the table from clients and contractors.

My path was not a straight line. After my B.Arch from Amity University Jaipur and a year-long internship in New Delhi, I worked in Jaipur, moved to Delhi and spent years inside different firms learning how practice really runs. I started Studio Dotbox when I received my COA licence and went fully independent in 2024. Along the way I completed a Master’s in Urban and Regional Planning, and I am now a doctoral researcher at GGSIPU.

I teach because almost everything that made me a better architect, I learnt after college. I want to build architects who lead, not just draw, grow the Indian architecture industry at home and abroad, and help people understand what an architect is really worth. The Grid is where that starts.

Questions

Before you join.

Who is The Grid for?

Architecture students from 1st year to fresh graduates, anywhere in India. Practising architects are not added to the student group.

Is joining The Grid free?

Yes. Fill in the registration form and you are added to the group after a quick check. Workshops and tracks are paid separately.

When do I get the masterclass link?

24 hours before the session, by email and in the masterclass WhatsApp group. You join the group straight after payment.

Will the masterclass be recorded?

Yes, for students who take the resources and recordings pack (₹99 at checkout). The recording stays available for 30 days.

Can I get a refund?

Seats are non-refundable. If a session is rescheduled, your seat moves to the new date automatically.

Which track should I pick?

Foundation for 1st and 2nd year, Core for 3rd and 4th year, Launch for 5th year and fresh graduates. Not sure? Write to studiodotbox@gmail.com with your year and college.

How do the paid tracks run?

Eight live 60 minute sessions on Microsoft Teams, in batches of up to 40. Assignments and feedback run on Google Classroom, and each batch has its own WhatsApp group.

What if I join a batch late or miss a session?

You get the recordings of the sessions held before you joined, so you can catch up before the next live class.

I am outside India. Can I join?

Yes. Write to studiodotbox@gmail.com before paying and we will share a payment option that works for you.
